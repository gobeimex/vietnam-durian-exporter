# vietnam-durian-exporter

GOBE IMEX — Golden Bee Import Export Co., Ltd — is a vietnam durian exporter with 12+ years of experience. We pack Ri6 and Monthong durian, fresh and frozen, in our own facilities in Tien Giang and Ho Chi Minh City, certified GLOBAL G.A.P., ISO 22000, FDA, Sedex and GRASP.

Our durian is harvested from 30+ hectares of our own orchards and 200+ hectares of partner farms in the Mekong Delta. Frozen durian is peeled and frozen within 12 hours of harvest, so the flesh stays thick, sweet and creamy. We ship whole fruit, peeled segments and pulp to North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia. Minimum order is 500 kg.

## Products

- Fresh durian — Ri6 & Monthong, whole fruit
- Frozen durian — peeled segments and pulp
- Durian paste for food manufacturers
